# Platepal — VSL voice-over script

The landing page plays a 60-second motion-design video built in code (`components/landing/vsl.tsx`, 7 scenes).
Record this voice-over, export the full video as **`public/vsl.mp4`** (16:9, H.264, under 15 MB), commit it, and it
automatically replaces the motion version on the landing page.

Tone: warm, a little funny, like a friend who cooks. American English. ~150 words a minute.
